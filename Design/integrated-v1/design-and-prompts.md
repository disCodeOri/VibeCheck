## Design integration

The supplied logo and brand kit guide the navy, cobalt and cloud-blue palette, lowercase wordmark and overlapping organic shapes. Blush provides the calm layout, photo-first hierarchy, encouraging tone, wardrobe and 3D preview. Brute provides oversized typography behind the user, prominent numerical scores, short verdicts and perforated ticket cards. The purple layered blobs are translated into dimensional blue shapes echoing the brand icon.

Retained details: portrait in front of display text; large score and verdict on a notched ticket; encouragement over the photo; overlapping translucent blob layers; portrait-specific callout pointers; complete avatar with alternate views, garment strip and “Drag to rotate”; clothing uploads and face/body model setup entry point.

For implementation, use the original brand assets rather than extracting the generated logo. The 3D view and rotation are visual concepts here; this image does not provide a model or interaction. Motion can later use gentle independent movement of the blob layers while keeping text fixed and respecting reduced-motion preferences.
